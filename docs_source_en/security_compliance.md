# 🛡️ Security, Privacy & Compliance Controls

Synalux provides workspace access controls and reviewable activity records that can support your organization’s privacy and compliance program. Your organization remains responsible for configuring access, reviewing activity, managing devices and integrations, and confirming that its policies meet applicable requirements.

## Review workspace activity

Authorized administrators can use the audit log to review recorded actions, filter the list, and inspect the available event details. What is recorded and how long it is retained depends on the workspace configuration and service agreement.

## Control access by role

Use role management to give staff access appropriate to their responsibilities. Review each role before assigning it and use the least access needed for the job.

## Customer responsibilities

- Review user access when staff join, change responsibilities, or leave.
- Protect account credentials and use the sign-in protections available to your organization.
- Review audit activity and investigate unexpected access or changes.
- Confirm retention, business-associate, subprocessor, and regulatory requirements with your Synalux representative before relying on them.
- Follow your organization’s procedures for device security, incident response, exports, and shared files.

The screens above show the current customer controls. They do not, by themselves, certify that a particular organization is compliant with HIPAA or another regulation.
