# Direct Messages and Team Chat

The current **Team** screen provides workspace channels for internal conversation and video-call controls. It also reserves a **Direct Messages** area, but the customer screen does not currently provide a control to create or search for a new direct-message thread.

## Use Team Chat today

1. Open **Team**.
2. Select an available workspace channel such as **general**, **clinical-team**, or **billing**.
3. Enter a message and choose **Send**. Voice input appears when the device and browser support it.
4. Use the video control only after confirming the participants, purpose, and any recording-consent prompt.

## Direct-message availability

Do not assume that an empty **Direct Messages** area is an error or that another user can be found from this screen. The current customer UI does not expose thread creation, participant selection, workspace-wide direct-message search, or patient mentions.

If your organization needs a private staff conversation, use an approved team channel or ask your administrator to confirm whether a direct-message workflow has been enabled for your account.

## Privacy

Use only the channels and participants authorized for the topic. Keep clinical details in the patient record whenever the record—not a chat thread—is the appropriate source of truth.
