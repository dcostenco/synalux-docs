# 📝 Clinical Notes & Documentation

Synalux combines dictation, structured note fields, review, and signature-request workflows in the clinical workspace. Clinicians remain responsible for reviewing every note and meeting payer, licensing, consent, and organizational requirements.

## Voice-assisted SOAP drafting

Use the SOAP workspace to enter or dictate source text and organize a draft into Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan sections. Confirm recording consent before using dictation. Review and correct the resulting text before it becomes part of the record.

## Structured note review

The note workspace keeps the sections and actions needed to review a clinical draft. Complete only fields relevant to the encounter, verify patient and encounter context, and sign only after the content is accurate.

## Signature requests

Authorized staff can review signature requests and their current status from the e-signature center. Confirm the recipient, document, and signing requirements before sending a request; then verify the completed document is associated with the intended record.

## Practice Drive

Practice Drive lists the documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and other supported files available to the signed-in user. File types, editing tools, sharing controls, and external connections depend on workspace configuration.

## Access and audit review

Restrict note access to appropriate roles and periodically review recorded workspace activity. The audit log shows events available to the administrator; it does not replace clinical review or your organization’s compliance procedures.
