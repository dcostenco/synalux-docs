# Scheduling & Appointments

Use **Schedule** to manage appointments across providers and locations.

## Calendar views and filters

Switch between day, week, and month views. Provider and location filters help staff focus on the schedule they are responsible for.

## Create or edit an appointment

1. Select **New Appointment** or open an existing appointment.
2. Choose the patient, provider, date, time, appointment type, and location.
3. Add notes or telehealth details when applicable.
4. Save the appointment.

Synalux warns about scheduling conflicts and authorization concerns found for the selected appointment. Staff can edit, complete, cancel, or mark an appointment as a no-show.

## Recurring visits

Staff can create daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly recurring visits. Synalux creates the dated appointments so each occurrence can be managed on the calendar.

## Patient portal requests

Patients can request an appointment from the Patient Portal and submit a reschedule request. Staff remain responsible for reviewing the request and maintaining the confirmed practice schedule.

## Waitlist and reminders

When an appointment is cancelled, Synalux can identify a matching waitlist candidate and alert staff for follow-up. Appointment reminders are available when outbound SMS and patient consent are configured. Delivery still depends on valid patient contact information and the practice's messaging setup.
