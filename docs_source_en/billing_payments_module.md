# 💳 Billing, Claims & Payments

The Synalux Billing workspace brings ready-to-bill items, claims, denials, accounts-receivable aging, recorded payments, and EDI batches into one staff workflow. Available payer, eligibility, ERA, payment, and invoice actions depend on the services configured for the workspace.

## Ready-to-bill review

Review the client, date of service, procedure code, units, charge, payer, and status before submission. The pre-submission check can identify configured validation issues, but billing staff remain responsible for documentation, coding, authorization, payer rules, and final submission.

## Claims and remittance workflow

- Open **Claims** to review claim status.
- Open **Denials** to work denied items using the information available to the workspace.
- Use **Upload ERA (835)** only for a remittance file from the intended payer and workspace.
- Review **A/R Aging**, **Payments**, and **EDI Batches** separately; one status does not prove another step completed.

## Eligibility, payments, and invoices

The top-level actions provide entry points for eligibility checks, recording a payment, and creating an invoice when those workflows are enabled. Verify the response or saved record after each action. Do not treat a button click as proof of payer eligibility, processor settlement, invoice delivery, or bank reconciliation.

## Accounting handoff

Users with finance access can review the separate accounting workspace. Currency and connected-service status are configured per workspace.

## Access and audit review

Limit billing and payment access to appropriate roles. Authorized administrators can review the workspace events available in the audit log. Your organization remains responsible for payer enrollment, coding, claim review, payment reconciliation, refunds, tax treatment, retention, and financial reporting.
