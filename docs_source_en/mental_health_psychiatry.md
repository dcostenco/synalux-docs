# 🧠 Mental Health & Psychiatry Workflows

Mental health teams can use Synalux clinical notes, configurable reports, telehealth, medication, billing, and audit workspaces. Qualified clinicians remain responsible for assessment, diagnosis, safety planning, treatment, escalation, and documentation.

## Clinical notes

Use the SOAP workspace to enter or dictate source text and organize a draft. Confirm consent before recording and review every section before signing. Do not rely on automated text as a crisis assessment or safety decision.

## Telehealth workspace

Use the telehealth area to review the meeting actions and scheduled sessions available to the workspace. Confirm patient identity, consent, privacy, emergency location, and your organization’s telehealth procedure before starting a clinical encounter.
