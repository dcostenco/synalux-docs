# Telehealth

Synalux Telehealth provides a browser-based video session for authorized participants. Availability depends on the workspace's configured media service and the user's access to the session.

## Find and join a session

1. Open **Telehealth**.
2. Sign in through the Patient Portal when prompted.
3. Review **Ready to join** and **Upcoming** sessions.
4. Enter a ready session and allow camera and microphone access.

A session becomes joinable shortly before its scheduled start. Users who are not recorded as participants are denied access. Staff can select **New session** from the lobby to continue to the scheduling workflow.

## In-call controls

The current one-to-one call interface can provide:

- Microphone mute and camera controls
- Screen sharing and annotation
- In-call text chat
- Picture-in-picture when the browser supports it
- Captions after the user accepts the speech-processing disclosure and consent is recorded
- Connection and TURN-fallback warnings

Browser, device, network, and media-service support affect which controls work. Test the exact customer setup before a clinical visit.

## Current recording and documentation boundary

The current Telehealth screen does not expose a call-recording control, recording-retention setting, or automatic recording-to-Drive workflow. It also does not automatically file every meeting, send every join link, or generate a signed clinical note. Keep the visit documentation in the approved Clinical Notes workflow and verify any invitation in the communication channel used to send it.

## Privacy and safety

Confirm participant identity, obtain the consent required by your organization, and use a private environment. Captions can send audio to a speech-processing service after disclosure; leave captions off when consent is not available. Telehealth controls support the organization's privacy program but do not by themselves establish regulatory compliance.
