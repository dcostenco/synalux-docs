# ⚠️ Allergies & Patient Warnings

Synalux keeps allergy information in the patient record and displays available warnings in that chart. Allergy records and banners support clinician review; they do not replace medication reconciliation, interaction checking, allergy verification, or clinical judgment.

## Patient-level allergy warning

The patient record shows the documented allergen and can surface a high-visibility warning when the record includes a severe allergy. Verify the patient, allergen, severity, reaction, source, and date before prescribing or administering treatment.

## Safe workflow

1. Confirm patient identity before opening the allergy tab.
2. Verify the allergy with the patient or authorized source.
3. Record the allergen, type, severity, and known reaction using the fields available to your role.
4. Reconcile medications and treatment plans independently.
5. Escalate discrepancies according to clinical policy rather than clearing a warning to continue.

## Access and audit review
