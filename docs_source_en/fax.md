# Fax

The current Synalux Fax screen sends outbound faxes through the organization’s configured fax service and keeps a history of those sends. It does not provide an inbound fax inbox.

## Send a fax

1. Open **Fax** and enter the recipient number in international format.
2. Attach one PDF, TIF, or TIFF file up to 25 MB, or provide an HTTPS document URL.
3. Add optional cover-page text.
4. Review the recipient and document, then choose **Send Fax**.
5. Wait for the confirmation that the fax was queued.

If the product reports that fax is not configured or unavailable for your subscription, contact your workspace administrator. Do not retry repeatedly or use an unapproved personal fax account.

## Track a send

**Fax History** shows the recipient, document name, status, pages when available, and date. Choose **Check** for a queued or sending fax, or **Refresh** to reload the history.

Queued is not the same as delivered. Confirm the final status before treating a records request, referral, or authorization as sent successfully.

## Privacy

Verify the recipient number and minimum necessary content before sending. Follow your organization’s cover-page, consent, retention, and incident-response procedures. Synalux does not automatically match an outbound fax to a patient record in this documented workflow.
