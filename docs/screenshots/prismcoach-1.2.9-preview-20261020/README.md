# PrismCoach screenshots, October 20, 2026

These iPhone captures were added on October 20, 2026. Six of them are also the
iPhone screenshots on PrismCoach's App Store listing
(https://apps.apple.com/app/id6778001709); on November 14, 2026, when the
listing showed version 1.2.14, the listing's images matched these files pixel
for pixel. Because they come from an earlier build, a few screens differ in
1.2.14: the workout screen's **Feedback** button is now **Rate & finish**, and
on iPhone the workout card on the Exercise guide tab shows **Details**, which
expands it to reach **How to perform this exercise**.

The PrismCoach guides show these six:

- `coach.png`: AI Coach with Plan a workout and suggested questions.
- `planning-pain.png`: Plan a workout with a recorded pain limitation.
- `workout-incline.png`: Active Workout, Exercise guide tab.
- `guide-incline-setup.png` and `guide-incline-move.png`: the two illustrated
  steps of How to perform this exercise.
- `workout-muscle-map.png`: Active Workout, Muscle map tab.

`home.png`, `muscles.png`, `nutrition.png` and `programs.png` are not shown in
the guides. No image implies live health measurements, a completed purchase or
a medically validated exercise choice.
