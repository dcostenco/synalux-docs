# Synalux apps on the App Store

These Synalux apps are available on the App Store. Each app's App Store page is the current source for its description, screenshots, privacy details, prices and in-app purchases.

| App | What it does | Runs on | Download | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Prism AAC** | Augmentative and alternative communication: tap pictures, build sentences and hear them spoken | iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch; Mac with Apple silicon (iPad version) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6764692277) | [Prism AAC](PRISMAAC.md) |
| **Prism AAC Browser** | Web browser for AAC users and healthcare providers, with AAC communication controls | iPhone, iPad, Mac | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6781468968) | [Prism Browser](docs_source_en/prism_browser.md) |
| **PrismCoach** | Strength, recovery, workout and nutrition coaching | iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch; Mac with Apple silicon (iPad version) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6778001709) | [PrismCoach](PRISMCOACH.md) |
| **Synalux POS** | Point of sale for restaurants and retail | iPhone, iPad; Mac with Apple silicon (iPad version) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6777999853) | [Synalux POS](docs_source_en/pos.md) |
| **Synalux OO** | Online ordering for restaurant customers: browse the menu, customize items and order for pickup or delivery | iPhone, iPad; Mac with Apple silicon (iPad version) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6778045142) | [Online Ordering](docs_source_en/pos.md#online-ordering--qr-table) |

## Requirements

- **Prism AAC, Prism AAC Browser, Synalux POS and Synalux OO:** iOS or iPadOS 16 or later. Prism AAC on Apple Watch needs watchOS 10 or later. On a Mac they need macOS 13 or later; the iPad versions also need an Apple M1 chip or later.
- **PrismCoach:** iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, and watchOS 10 or later on Apple Watch. On a Mac it needs macOS 15 or later and an Apple M1 chip or later.

On a Mac with Apple silicon, an app marked "iPad version" above is the iPad app; Apple lists it as designed for iPad.
